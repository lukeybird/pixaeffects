# Pixa Launcher attribution

The launcher interface and installer are original Pixa code. The sixteen original Pixa pixel product marks are reused from this repository's website icon set. The launcher P mark is generated by package_macos.py. No Adobe artwork is included.

Runtime dependencies: eframe/egui (MIT OR Apache-2.0), reqwest (MIT OR Apache-2.0), fs2 (MIT OR Apache-2.0), plist (MIT), tempfile (MIT OR Apache-2.0), serde/serde_json (MIT OR Apache-2.0), sha2/hex/ed25519-dalek (per their included license notices). Their resolved transitive license notices are in THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt. eframe's default fonts carry the Ubuntu Font Licence 1.0 and SIL Open Font License 1.1; their notices are included there.

Each editor remains a separate app with its own upstream license and notices. Installing through the launcher does not change those licenses. No signing key or credential is included.
